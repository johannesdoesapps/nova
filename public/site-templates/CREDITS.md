# Website template assets

Photos are from [StockSnap](https://stocksnap.io), released under CC0 (no rights reserved), found through [Openverse](https://openverse.org). Each was cropped and converted to WebP for these templates. Names, people, prices and quotes in the templates are sample content, not real people or businesses.

Fonts (in `fonts/`) are the latin subsets of Plus Jakarta Sans, Instrument Serif, DM Sans, Fraunces, Jost, Newsreader and Inter from [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
