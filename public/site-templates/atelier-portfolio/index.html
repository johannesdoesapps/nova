The studio

We're a small team of designers, writers and strategists who believe the best work comes from curiosity, craft and candour.

Founded in 2016 by Elias Nord, the studio partners with cultural institutions, founders and independent brands to shape how they look, sound and feel. We keep our team small on purpose, so every client works directly with the people doing the work.

Elias Nord, Founder & Creative Director
